Common Questions

Is the in-home visit really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the home with you and give you a written plan and quote.

Do you supply the products or just install them?

Both. We supply and install, so you have one company responsible for the whole job.

Will it look like a hospital?

No. We focus on high-end, custom finishes that match your home, like grab bars that look like towel bars.

Can you do one room at a time?

Yes. Many people start with the bathroom and add more later. Your plan shows what to do first.
