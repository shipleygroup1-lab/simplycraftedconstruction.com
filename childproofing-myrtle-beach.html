Common Questions

Is the in-home visit really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the home with you and give you a written plan and quote.

Why not just buy gates at the store?

Many stairs, banisters and wide openings don't fit a standard gate, and a pressure gate isn't meant for the top of stairs. We measure, choose the right product and mount it properly.

Will it damage my walls or railings?

We pick mounting options to fit your home and install them carefully. We'll go over the options during the visit.

Do you supply the products?

Yes. We supply and install, so you have one company responsible for the whole job.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Simply Crafted Construction LLC holds South Carolina Specialty Contractor License #63242 and is insured.
