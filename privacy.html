Privacy Policy

Simply Crafted Construction LLC · Last updated November 12, 2026

Simply Crafted Construction LLC ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us, request a quote, or fill out one of our forms (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address, city and zip code.
  • Details about your project, such as the type and size of building you are interested in.
  • Anything else you choose to tell us when you call, text, email or message us.

How we use it

  • To call, text or email you about the quote or project you asked about.
  • To prepare estimates, schedule site visits and complete your project.
  • To keep records of our work with you.

If you reply STOP to a text message from us, we will stop texting you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We only share it with the people and tools we need to serve you: our customer management and scheduling software, our bookkeeping software, and suppliers or manufacturers involved in your project (for example, a metal building manufacturer that produces and delivers your building). We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to serve you and to meet business and legal record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you at any time by contacting us below.
